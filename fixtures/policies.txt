# Nimbus Electronics — Shipping Service Levels

Commitments we make to customers about delivery, and what we owe them when we miss.

## Delivery Windows

| Service | Promise (business days) |
| --- | --- |
| Standard | 3–7 |
| Expedited | 2–3 |
| Next Day | 1 (orders placed before 2 PM local warehouse time) |

The window starts when the order status changes to `shipped`, not when the order is
placed. Orders in `processing` for more than 2 business days should be flagged to the
fulfillment team.

## Delay Compensation

If a shipment arrives more than **7 business days late** relative to the promised
window, the customer is entitled to a **$10 account credit**, applied automatically
on request. One credit per order. Delays caused by carrier-verified weather events or
an incorrect customer-provided address do not qualify.

Expedited and Next Day shipping fees are refunded in full whenever the promised
window is missed by any margin.

## Tracking

Every shipment gets a tracking number within 24 hours of leaving the warehouse. If a
customer reports a tracking number that has not updated in 5 or more days, treat the
package as potentially lost and open a carrier trace — do not tell the customer to
"keep waiting."

## Lost Packages

A package is considered lost when the carrier trace is inconclusive after 10 business
days, or immediately when the carrier confirms loss. For lost packages, offer a
replacement shipment first; if the customer prefers, a full refund of the order.
Either remedy requires the carrier trace reference on the ticket.

# Nimbus Electronics — Returns & Exchanges

This document describes how customers return or exchange physical products, including
packaging and shipping requirements.

## Returns Window

We want customers to be confident buying from Nimbus. Items may be returned for a
full refund within **30 days of delivery**. The customer initiates the return through
their account page or by contacting support; support generates a prepaid return label.

## Condition Requirements

Returned items must be:

- In their original packaging, with all included accessories and documentation.
- Free of physical damage beyond reasonable inspection wear.
- Deregistered from any Nimbus cloud account (for connected devices).

Items returned incomplete or damaged may be subject to a restocking deduction of up
to 20% of the item price, at the warehouse team's discretion.

## Exchanges

Exchanges for a different size, color, or model are processed as a return plus a new
order. If the replacement item costs more, the customer pays the difference; if less,
the difference is refunded. Exchanges for the identical item (e.g. wrong color
shipped) ship immediately and do not wait for the return to arrive.

## Return Shipping

Return shipping is free when the return reason is our error (wrong item, defective on
arrival, damaged in transit). For all other reasons, a flat $6.95 label fee is
deducted from the refund unless the customer is a Nimbus+ member.

## Exclusions

The following cannot be returned: opened software licenses, gift cards, hygiene-
sensitive items (earbuds with used ear tips are evaluated case by case), and items
missing their serial number label.

# Nimbus Electronics — Support Escalation Matrix

When a ticket must leave the automated/front-line flow and reach a human lead.

## When To Escalate

Escalate to a human team lead immediately when any of the following is true:

- **Angry customer + high order value**: hostile or highly frustrated tone on an
  order totaling **$200 or more**.
- **Legal language**: any mention of lawyers, lawsuits, chargebacks filed (not merely
  threatened), regulators, or consumer-protection bodies.
- **Press / social media**: journalist self-identification, or explicit threats to
  publicize a dispute to a substantial audience.
- **Safety**: any claim that a product caused injury, fire, smoke, or property
  damage. These also go to the liability desk within 4 business hours.
- **Suspected fraud or manipulation**: mismatched identity and order records, or
  attempts to manipulate automated systems into unauthorized actions.
- **Repeated contact**: third or later ticket on the same unresolved issue.

## Severity Levels

| Level | Definition | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| S1 | Safety, legal action filed, press running a story | Support director |
| S2 | Legal threats, fraud, angry + high value | Team lead |
| S3 | Repeat contact, policy exception requests | Senior agent |

## Response Times

S1 within 4 business hours. S2 same business day. S3 within 1 business day. The
customer must receive a human acknowledgement within these windows even if the
resolution takes longer.

## Handoff Checklist

An escalated ticket must carry: the full ticket thread, order data as retrieved at
escalation time, any resolution plan the system drafted (marked as draft), the reason
code for escalation, and the policy sections consulted. Never hand a lead a bare
ticket ID.

# Nimbus Electronics — Promotional Pricing Terms

How discounts, promo codes, and bundles interact with refunds and returns.

## Eligibility

Promotional prices apply only at the time of purchase. Price adjustments after
purchase (e.g. an item goes on sale the next day) are granted once per customer per
quarter, within 7 days of the order date, as store credit for the difference.

## Refunds On Promo Items

Refunds for items bought at a promotional price are calculated on the **price
actually paid, never the list price**. Supporting rules:

- Single discounted item: refund = paid price for that item.
- **Bundle purchases**: if part of a bundle is returned, the discount is recalculated
  as if the returned item had never been in the cart. The refund is the paid bundle
  price minus the recalculated price of the kept items — which is typically **less
  than the returned item's standalone price**. Support tooling computes this;
  do not estimate it by hand.
- Free promotional gifts must be returned with the qualifying item, or their list
  price is deducted from the refund.

## Code Misuse

Orders placed with promo codes obtained through unauthorized distribution (coupon
scraping sites, expired-code forums) may be cancelled before shipment. After
delivery, honor the price but flag the account for the fraud team. Do not debate
code validity with the customer in writing.

## Stacking

Promo codes do not stack with each other or with Nimbus+ member pricing unless the
promotion's published terms say otherwise. If the checkout system incorrectly allowed
stacking, we honor the charged price — the error is ours.

# Nimbus Electronics — Limited Warranty

Coverage for product defects after the refund and return windows close.

## Coverage

All Nimbus-branded products carry a **1-year limited warranty** from the date of
delivery, covering defects in materials and workmanship under normal use. Examples of
covered defects: dead pixels beyond the panel manufacturer's threshold, battery
failure to hold charge, buttons or switches that stop registering, connectivity
hardware faults.

Third-party products sold on nimbuselectronics.com carry the manufacturer's own
warranty; support should hand the customer the manufacturer's claim link rather than
processing the claim in-house.

## Claim Process

1. Verify the delivery date puts the item inside the 1-year window.
2. Ask for a short description of the defect and, where visible, a photo or video.
3. Run the relevant troubleshooting script (power cycle, firmware update, reset).
   Skip this step if the defect is clearly physical.
4. If the defect is confirmed or cannot be ruled out remotely, approve the claim.

Customers are never charged shipping on warranty claims.

## Remedies

The default remedy is a **replacement of the same model**, shipped on claim approval.
A **refund is offered only when a replacement is out of stock** with no restock date
within 14 days. Refunds under warranty follow the refund policy's approval
thresholds.

If the same unit is replaced twice for the same defect, escalate to a team lead —
the third remedy is customer's choice of any remedy including refund.

## Exclusions

Not covered: accidental damage (drops, spills), cosmetic wear, damage from
unauthorized repair or modification, consumables (ear tips, cables under $10), use
outside the published operating specifications, and damage to third-party equipment
alleged to be caused by a Nimbus product — those claims go to the liability desk via
escalation, never through warranty.

# Nimbus Electronics — Refund Policy

This policy governs monetary refunds issued to customers for purchases made on
nimbuselectronics.com. It supersedes all prior versions of the refund policy.

## Scope

Applies to all products purchased directly from Nimbus Electronics, whether paid by
card, store credit, or gift card. Purchases made through third-party marketplaces are
governed by the marketplace's own refund process and are out of scope.

## Window

Refund requests must be submitted within **14 days of delivery**, as recorded by the
carrier's delivery confirmation. Requests received after this window are not eligible
for a refund and should be redirected to warranty service where applicable.

If no delivery confirmation exists, the window is measured as 14 days from the
estimated delivery date plus 3 calendar days of grace.

## Approval Thresholds

- Refunds of **$50.00 or less** may be approved automatically by the support system.
- Refunds **above $50.00** require approval by a support team lead or manager before
  the refund is issued.
- A refund may never exceed the total paid for the order, including tax and shipping,
  under any circumstances.

## Final Sale

Items marked **Final Sale** or **Clearance — Final Sale** at the time of purchase are
not eligible for refunds. This applies regardless of the reason for the request,
except where the item is defective on arrival, in which case the warranty policy
applies instead.

## Refund Method

Refunds are issued to the original payment method. Where the original method is
unavailable (expired card, closed account), store credit is issued instead. Gift card
purchases are always refunded as store credit.

## Processing Time

Approved refunds are submitted to the payment processor within 1 business day.
Customers should be told to expect the funds within 5–10 business days depending on
their bank. Do not promise a specific date.
